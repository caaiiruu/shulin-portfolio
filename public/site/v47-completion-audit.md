# Portfolio v47 — recruiter-role QA and responsive audit

## Root causes fixed

- Removed fixed viewport chapter ownership from Search and Domain.
- Rebuilt Mobile Domain as a compact header plus normal-flow reading card.
- Replaced the cramped Business/Team Impact half-and-half layout with sequential sections.
- Team Impact now shows function then changed behaviour; it never uses a narrow text column.
- Standardised every horizontal rail to 12px gap and 12px edge inset.
- Added mobile-specific dialog typography, panel padding, and section stacking.
- Reduced popup close-control overlap by reserving a safe right area.
- Preserved Financial services as the initial Domain.
- Strengthened popup scroll reset before and after switching cases.

## QA by hiring role

### Recruiter
- First-screen titles remain concise and scannable.
- Mobile Domain no longer begins with a three-line oversized statement.
- Cards retain Role, Scale, Outcome or context-specific evidence.

### Design hiring manager
- Key decisions remain readable at full width.
- What made this hard appears before decisions and evidence.
- Interaction and responsive states have component ownership.

### Product manager
- Business impact is separated from team adoption.
- Domain conclusions and design responses remain visible without nested scrolling.

### Engineering partner
- Ownership, delivery, shared-state logic, and reusable-system evidence remain explicit.
- Breakpoint behavior and rail spacing are tokenized.

### Accessibility / frontend reviewer
- Mobile reflow avoids horizontal page overflow by design.
- Focus states remain external and visible.
- Native Domain select is retained on compact widths.
- Real browser harness covers Chromium and WebKit.
