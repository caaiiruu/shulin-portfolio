# Evidence Rules
Evidence = why decisions were credible.
Supports image, numeric, and structured non-image evidence.
Structured non-image groups: heading + concise supporting text/bullets.
Do not force text evidence into metric cards or fake image placeholders.
Semantic redaction only; preserve image structure and hide sensitive content.

## Validation-scale layer

Structured Evidence may lead with compact, source-supported validation breadth before decision-support findings while preserving Evidence → Decision mapping.
