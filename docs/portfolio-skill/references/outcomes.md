# Outcome Rules
Business Impact is integrated into Outcomes.
Quantitative projects: summary + large metrics + supporting statement + optional system closing.
Qualitative projects: optional section headline + qualitative cards + optional closing scope/measurement statement.
Never fabricate KPI.
Research/delivery counts are Evidence, not Outcomes.

## Aligned metrics and team recognition

Quantitative Outcomes may use an aligned metric grid. NPS may be primary when the response count is sourced. Team recognition must remain subordinate and must not be presented as an individual award.
